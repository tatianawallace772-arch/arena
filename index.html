How the plant works

This is a roast–leach–electrowin (RLE) zinc refinery. Sphalerite concentrate is oxidized, dissolved in recycled sulfuric acid, purified with zinc dust, then plated as special-high-grade metal.

Circuit

  • Roaster — 900–950 °C fluid bed. Too cold leaves sulfide; too hot sinters calcine.
  • Acid plant — SO₂ is converted to H₂SO₄ and returned to leach and cells.
  • Leach — Neutral pH ~4.8 dissolves ZnO while holding iron in residue.
  • Purification — Zinc dust cements Cu and Cd. Leftover metals wreck current efficiency.
  • Cellhouse — ~500 A/m², 3.3 V. Faraday’s law plus hydrogen evolution sets production.
  • Casting — Cathodes are melted and poured as SHG ingots.
